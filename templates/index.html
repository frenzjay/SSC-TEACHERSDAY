GUIDE

  • Step 1: Connect Venue Display - Press Win + P on your keyboard and select "Extend" to output to the venue's LED wall or projector.
  • Step 2: Launch LED Wall Display - Click "LAUNCH STAGE SCREEN". Drag the popup window over to the extended LED wall screen, and click the FULLSCREEN toggle (or press F11).
  • Step 3: Open Operator Dashboard - Keep the Controller open on your laptop screen. Everything you trigger will synchronize sub-millisecond with the LED wall via the offline game_show_bus channel.
  • Step 4: Sound Check - By default, stage sound effects (chimes, buzzer, drumrolls) synthesize procedurally in your browser without requiring external audio files. Hit "1" (Correct) or "2" (Wrong) to test venue speakers.
